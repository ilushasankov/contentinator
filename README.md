## 📲 Installation Guide

The application is distributed as an APK file directly from the developer.

1. **Download APK**: Click the **Download** button above.
2. **Allow Installation**: When opening the file, go to **Settings** -> **"Allow from this source"**.
3. **Complete Setup**: Confirm the action and enjoy!

> **Requirements:** Android 12.0+ (API 31+). File size ~7.4 MB.

## 🛡 Security and Privacy

This policy applies to Contentinator, developed by Ilya Sankov. See the [full Privacy Policy](./privacy.html#en).

1. **Data collection and use:** We do not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers.
2. **Local storage:** All data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
3. **AI interaction:** Requests are sent directly to AI providers using your personal keys. The developer has no access to them.
4. **Announcements and updates:** Announcements, news, and partner offers are created and published by the Contentinator developer.
