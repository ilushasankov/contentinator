Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Contentinator application, developed by Ilya Sankov.

1. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers.

2. Local Data Storage

All data is stored only on your device. When you uninstall the app, the data is deleted.

3. AI Interaction

Requests are sent directly to AI providers using your personal keys. The developer does not have access to them.

4. Announcements and Updates

All announcements, updates, and partner offers are created and published personally by the Contentinator developer. To receive them, the app periodically connects to a protected GitHub Gist. We still do not collect your personal data; these requests transmit only standard technical parameters required for network communication.
