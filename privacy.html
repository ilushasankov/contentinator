Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Contentinator mobile application, developed by Ilya Sankov.

1. Data Collection and Use

Contentinator follows a "privacy-first" principle: we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers.

  • Personal Information: We do not request your name, email, or phone number.
  • Technical Data: The app does not use third-party analytics services or advertising identifiers.

2. Local Data Storage

All your ideas, scripts, and notes are stored exclusively in a local database on your device. Data is not synchronized with the developer's cloud. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete your data unless you have performed a manual backup.

3. AI Interaction

The app uses third-party AI provider APIs (OpenAI, OpenRouter, etc.). In this case:

  • Data (prompts) is sent directly to the provider via your personal API key.
  • The developer has no access to your API keys or conversation history.
